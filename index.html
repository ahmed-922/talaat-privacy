Privacy policy

Talaat helps you discover places and plan outings in Bahrain. This policy explains what information we use and how to contact us about it. Talaat is operated by Ahmed Abdulrahman Ali Bahar and the Talaat team.

Information we collect

  • Account information: Your name and email address. Firebase Authentication handles email and password sign-in. If you use Google sign-in, we receive information Google makes available, such as your name and email address.
  • Profile details: Your country and whether you are a local or visiting Bahrain. You may also provide a location or city and phone number. The location field is information you enter.
  • Your activity: Your saved places and plans, including their places and dates.
  • Technical information: Our service providers may process your IP address, device or browser information, and authentication activity to operate and secure their services.
  • Device storage: Talaat may store account-related information and cached data on your device for sign-in, performance, and synchronization.

An account, your name, country, and whether you are a local or visitor are required during registration. You can leave the optional location and phone number blank.

How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, sign you in, maintain your profile, synchronize saved places and plans, operate Talaat, respond to support requests, and meet legal obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We use Google Firebase for authentication, database, storage, and hosting. Google processes information needed to provide these services. If you choose Google sign-in, Google also processes information under its own policy. We may disclose information when required by law or a valid legal order.

See Firebase privacy and security and the Google Privacy Policy.

International processing

Information may be processed outside Bahrain. Google states that Firebase Authentication processes data in the United States; other Firebase services may use different locations.

Retention and deletion

Account information, saved places, and plans are kept while your account is active. To delete your account, open Profile → Account → Delete account, confirm that you understand, and verify your sign-in. Talaat then deletes your profile, saved places, plans, and Talaat sign-in account. Deleting Talaat does not delete your Google account.

A deletion marker containing your account identifier remains in our database to prevent account data from being recreated during deletion. Its cleanup and retention period have not yet been finalized. Your device and service providers may retain cached data, backups, or technical records; their retention periods are still being verified.

Your choices and rights

You can update available profile information in the app. Contact us to ask about access, correction, or other rights under applicable Bahrain law. We may need to verify your identity.

Security

We use technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. No system is completely secure.

Other services

Talaat may link to maps, menus, websites, or other services with their own privacy practices.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact talaaatbh@gmail.com. We may update this policy as our practices change.
